# Quantitative Finance Books

Directory for full-length textbooks and monographs on quantitative equity portfolio management, factor investing, and statistical arbitrage.

## Recommended Foundational Texts for Alpha Research

| Title | Author(s) | Year | Publisher / Venue | Classification | Relevance to WorldQuant BRAIN Alpha Research |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| *Active Portfolio Management: A Quantitative Approach for Providing Superior Returns and Controlling Risk* (2nd ed.) | Richard C. Grinold & Ronald N. Kahn | 1999 | McGraw-Hill | Academic / Industry Monograph | Defines Information Ratio (IR), Fundamental Law of Active Management ($IR \approx IC \cdot \sqrt{BR}$), factor neutralization, and turnover-cost tradeoffs |
| *Finding Alphas: A Quantitative Approach to Building Trading Strategies* (2nd ed.) | Igor Tulchinsky et al. (WorldQuant) | 2019 | Wiley | Official / Industry Monograph | Authored by WorldQuant founder and researchers; directly covers Alpha design, decay, neutralization, universe selection, and avoiding overfitting on BRAIN |
| * Quantitative Equity Portfolio Management: Modern Techniques and Applications* | Edward E. Qian, Ronald H. Hua & Eric H. Sorensen | 2007 | Chapman & Hall/CRC | Academic / Industry Monograph | Deep coverage of value, quality, growth, analyst revision, and momentum factors, plus cross-sectional z-scoring and rank transformations |
| *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* | Marcos López de Prado | 2018 | Wiley | Academic / Industry Monograph | Deflated Sharpe Ratio, multiple-testing/backtest overfitting pitfalls, and feature importance |
